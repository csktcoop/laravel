# Requirements and Analysis

## Purpose

Provide a structured methodology for capturing and analyzing business needs, user requirements, and acceptance criteria.

## Goals

- Ensure clarity of scope.
- Align stakeholders on objectives.
- Define measurable acceptance criteria.

## Process

1. Requirement Gathering

   - Conduct stakeholder interviews.
   - Collect user stories and use cases.
   - Document functional and non-functional requirements.

1. Analysis

   - Identify dependencies and constraints.
   - Prioritize requirements using MoSCoW (Must, Should, Could, Won’t).
   - Validate requirements against business goals.

1. Deliverables

   - Requirements Specification Document (RSD).
   - Acceptance Criteria Matrix.
   - Traceability Matrix linking requirements to implementation and tests.
